<aufbau-proof> — interactive proof editor

Each editor below checks its proof against a fixed theory in the browser, debounced as you type. Try breaking a proof line to see the diagnostics, then fix it to watch it go green.

1. Standalone cell (theory + lemma in one element)

The theory is just axioms/terms; the cell is a self-contained lemma and only its proof body is editable.

2. A progression — later cells cite earlier ones

Cells that share a theory form one document, stitched in page order. The second cell proves main by citing the helper lemma from the first cell. Break the first cell's proof and note the second cell is not red-lined — it just stops being "verified" until the whole document is clean again.

3. A theorem cell (appends a declaration to the mm0)

A theorem cell contributes an mm0 theorem declaration and its proof, both stitched into the document. This is the seam a future editable mm0/def cell would use.

4. A real theory by URL (full-file mode)

A multi-theorem fixture loaded from src — the whole proof is editable, no single goal display.
